Fake Charity Donation Page: A Crisis Used as Bait

A fictional donation campaign borrows the emotional language of emergency relief while giving visitors too few ways to verify who receives the money.

EDITORIAL NOTE
This is a fictional demonstration case. The campaign name, interface and event shown below are invented for this article.

Emotion can hide missing information

Donation scams often work because the visitor wants to help quickly. A dramatic story, a large “Donate Now” button and a sense of urgency can distract from basic questions about the organization, its registration, the destination of funds and the payment processor.

Before donating, verify the charity independently. Search for the organization yourself rather than relying on links or contact details supplied by the appeal.

What we would investigate

We would verify the organization, compare its official domain with the campaign URL, inspect the payment destination and look for transparent information about who operates the campaign.
