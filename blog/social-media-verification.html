Social Media Verification Scam: The Blue Badge Bait

A fictional verification offer promises a trusted badge, then shifts the conversation toward login credentials and a payment method.

EDITORIAL NOTE
This is a fictional demonstration case. The account and interface below are created for scam-analysis purposes.

Trust signals can be copied

A verification badge is a powerful visual shortcut: it suggests identity, legitimacy and status. That makes the idea useful to scammers even when the offer itself comes from an unrelated website or message.

The key question is not whether the page looks professional. It is whether the offer can be verified inside the platform’s official account tools without following an external link supplied by a stranger.

What we would investigate

We would compare the offer with the platform’s official verification process, inspect the domain, check where the login form submits data, and look for payment requests that are not part of the genuine workflow.
