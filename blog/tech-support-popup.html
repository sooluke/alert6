Fake Tech Support Pop-Up: Your Device Is Infected

A fictional browser warning imitates a security notification and tries to turn a frightening pop-up into a phone call with fake support.

EDITORIAL NOTE
This is a fictional demonstration case. The support number and interface shown below are intentionally fake and are not allegations against a real company.

Why the pop-up looks convincing

Fake support scams often imitate the visual language of operating-system warnings: alarming colors, technical wording, repeated alerts and a command to call immediately. The design is meant to interrupt normal thinking.

A website cannot reliably diagnose a full device compromise simply because a browser page says it has detected one. Treat unsolicited support numbers as a major warning sign, especially when the page tells you not to close the browser.

What we would investigate

We would record the exact page URL, inspect redirects, check whether the warning is only browser content, and verify any claimed support channel independently. We would never install remote-access software at the request of an unsolicited pop-up.
