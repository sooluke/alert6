Fake Job Verification Scam: The Final Interview Fee

A fictional recruitment portal claims an applicant has passed screening, then introduces a small verification fee before the final interview.

EDITORIAL NOTE
This is a fictional demonstration case created for the Scam Alert website. The names, messages and interfaces shown below are invented and are not presented as real-world allegations.

The offer is designed to feel almost complete

The fictional portal tells the applicant that screening is already approved. The final step is presented as a routine identity or processing check, making the payment request appear administrative rather than suspicious.

A legitimate hiring process should not depend on a surprise payment

We would verify the employer independently, check the recruiter’s domain and compare the role with the company’s official careers page. Any unexpected fee should be treated as a major warning sign.

Pressure increases after the visitor has invested time

The strongest manipulation is psychological: after submitting an application and receiving apparent approval, the visitor may feel motivated to complete the last step. That sunk-cost feeling should never replace independent verification.
