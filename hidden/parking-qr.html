Fake Parking QR Scam: The Payment Code Trap

A fictional parking notice uses a QR code to send drivers to a payment page that looks official but is controlled by an unverified destination.

EDITORIAL NOTE
This is a fictional demonstration case created for the Scam Alert website. The names, messages and interfaces shown below are invented and are not presented as real-world allegations.

A QR code hides the destination until after the scan

The fictional sign gives the driver very little context before the scan. Once the QR code is opened, the visitor may see a polished payment page without realizing which domain they reached.

The safest check is the destination domain

Before paying, verify the parking operator independently and inspect the domain shown after scanning. If the payment page is unrelated to the operator or uses an unexpected domain, stop.

Convenience is part of the manipulation

Parking scams work because drivers are often standing outside, in a hurry and focused on avoiding a ticket. The safest approach is to use the official parking app, machine or website rather than trusting an isolated QR code.
