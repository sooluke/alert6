Privacy Policy

Scam Alert is a static investigative publication. This page explains the limited information handled when you use the report form.

Information you submit

If you use Report a Scam, you may provide a website URL, scam category, optional email address, investigation details and an optional evidence URL. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, identity documents or other sensitive information.

Why we receive it

Submitted information is used to review and document suspected scams. We do not need an account to browse the public website.

Form provider

The report form is designed to use Formspree as the form-processing service. When the form is connected, submitted data is transmitted to that service and handled according to its current policies and terms. Review the provider's documentation before activating the form.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not intentionally require advertising cookies or user accounts. If analytics or third-party embeds are added later, this policy should be updated before they are enabled.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact page. This policy is a general project notice and is not legal advice.
